Hinge math

The new door is leaning against the wall, the old hinges look tired, and the packet at the hardware store comes in twos and threes with no explanation. Hinge math turns the door's weight and height into a hinge count, spreads them down the stile, and counts the screws and packs. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Hinge count - door weight and height become a hinge count on labeled weight bands, with the tall-door bump.
  • Hinge placement - door height and count become positions from the top, on labeled offsets with even middles.
  • Screw plan - hinges become total screws, packs to buy, and the spares left over.

Labeled norms

Labeled illustrative weight bands: up to 18 kg takes 2 hinges, up to 40 kg takes 3, up to 60 kg takes 4, heavier takes 5; a door over 230 cm tall earns one extra hinge, up to 5. Labeled placement offsets: 18 cm from the top, 28 cm from the bottom, middles spread evenly. Labeled screw typical: 8 screws per hinge (4 per leaf), sold in packs you choose. These are reference values, not rules - heavy or fire-rated doors follow their own hardware schedules.
